# AI-SDLC Registry

> Generated by `aisdlc.mjs registry sync`. Do not edit by hand; edit artifact frontmatter and re-sync.
>
> Unfinished goals and every ADR. Completed and cancelled goals are in `registry-archive.md`: search it, don't read it whole.
> Owner is a goal's current owner, or who decided an ADR. Each goal's `decisions.md` logs who did what, and `.aisdlc/decisions.md` logs project-wide decisions.
> A goal's folder and tasks: `aisdlc.mjs goal show <G-id>`. ADR files: `adr/<ADR-id>-*.md`. Governance rules: `governance.md`.
